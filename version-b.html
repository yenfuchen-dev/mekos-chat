Why did Corridor bookings dip?

Client-level thread · no project, no skills armed

Corridor bookings were down 6% last week. Before I take this to the client — what actually happened?
✳MekOS No skills · reading the workspace

Three things moved that week. Only one of them is ours.

  • Not us. A 90-minute fault on the booking path on Tuesday the 14th. Ops logged it, and it shows in the workbook as failed sessions — not as lost demand.
  • Partly us. Paid social was dark Wednesday to Saturday, between flights. Four days with no support behind the route.
  • Mostly them. The DCA–LGA shuttle dropped fares the same week. Second time this quarter.

Strip the outage out and the real dip is nearer 4%, and about two-thirds of that tracks the dark days. I wouldn't lead with the fault — it invites a conversation about reliability you don't want inside a bookings review.
